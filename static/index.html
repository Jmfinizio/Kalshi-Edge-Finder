Kalshi vs Sportsbook

… Kalshi: – Books: – Odds API credits: –

Sportsbooks are treated as the true price (margin removed). An opportunity is a Kalshi contract that is cheaper than that fair price after Kalshi's fee: net edge = book fair probability − price you pay − fee. Buy the side the books favor. Nothing here places orders: use Paper bet to track it.

Price = the cheapest ask, before fees. All-in = price + Kalshi's fee per contract, which is what the order ticket really totals to. Size @ price = how many contracts are offered at that price (we assume you buy up to 100). Past that size the next cents cost more: click a row and use Live order cost.

Every Kalshi market we could match to a sportsbook game, sorted by the raw gap between book fair probability and Kalshi's midpoint. A big gap here is not necessarily a trade: check spread, size, fees and book age in the flags column.

Live and pregame are separate regimes: judge them separately.
